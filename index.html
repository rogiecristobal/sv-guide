EARLY GAME

Spring Year 1 — Laying the Foundation

🎯 Goals & Priorities

  • ✓ Unlock basic tools & clear a small farming area near the farmhouse
  • ✓ Establish stable money from crops (parsnips → potatoes) and fishing
  • ✓ Reach at least level 5–10 in the mines to unlock elevator checkpoints
  • ✓ Meet all villagers and start building friendships (2+ hearts with a few)
  • ✓ Begin Community Center bundles or Joja membership path

📋 First 2–3 Weeks

✅ DO THIS:

  • • Hoard all resources (wood, stone, fiber)
  • • Focus energy on a few good crops
  • • Fish when crops are watered
  • • Visit mines on rainy days

❌ AVOID:

  • • Overspending at Pierre's
  • • Clearing the entire farm early
  • • Ignoring the TV (weather & luck)
  • • Staying out past midnight

📝 Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your First Farm Plot

  1. 1 Clear a small rectangle (about 6×9 tiles) right next to your farmhouse using your axe and pickaxe. Save the wood and stone!
  2. 2 Hoe the soil in 3×3 blocks, leaving the center tile un-hoed. This pattern will perfectly fit a quality sprinkler later.
  3. 3 Water each tilled tile with your watering can. Stand in the center (the un-hoed tile) to reach all 8 surrounding tiles efficiently.
  4. 4 Plant your seeds — start with Parsnips (4 days, cheap) then switch to Potatoes for better profit. Place a Scarecrow nearby once you have 15+ crops.
  5. 5 Place a chest right outside your farmhouse door to store tools, seeds, and forage items — saves huge time!
Upgrade your Watering Can on the 27th of Spring (check TV for rain on the 28th). Water crops on the 27th, drop the can at Clint's, rain waters on the 28th, and you pick it up on the 29th — zero missed watering days!
Spring Onions grow in the southeast forest (near the sewer pipe) and are free energy! Check daily — they're great for early mine runs when you need extra stamina.
Don't donate your first Prismatic Shard or Dinosaur Egg to the museum! The shard unlocks the Galaxy Sword, and the egg can be incubated for unlimited dinosaurs. Keep them safe!

🍄 Foraging

Pick up forageables for easy early income and energy. Each season brings unique wild items.

🌸 Spring: Daffodil, Dandelion, Leek, Wild Horseradish ☀️ Summer: Spice Berry, Grape, Sweet Pea 🍂 Fall: Wild Plum, Hazelnut, Blackberry

Best spots: Forest south of the farm, bus stop area, mountain paths. Check daily — they respawn!

🎣 Fishing

The fishing mini-game takes practice — keep the fish inside the green bar by tapping (not holding) the button.

↑ Green bar = your catch zone | Red dot = the fish

Best early spots: Mountain Lake (carp are easy), River in town. Buy the Training Rod from Willy for easier catches!

⛏️ Mining

Aim to descend 5 floors per trip to unlock the next elevator checkpoint. Don't break every single rock!

⬆ Ladder under a rock — break strategically! Yellow = copper ore

Strategy: Use bombs on dense rock clusters. Bring food! Upgrade pickaxe at Clint's as soon as you can afford it.

⚔️ Combat

Timing is everything — swing your sword just as the monster enters range. Block with right-click to reduce damage.

🗡️

Green Slime: Easy, just swing!

Rock Crab: Wait for it to peek, then strike.

Bat: Time your swing as it swoops in.

Upgrade your weapon at the Adventurer's Guild. Rings with glow or magnetism are huge quality-of-life boosts!

MID GAME

Late Year 1 to Year 2 — Building & Expanding

🎯 Mid-Game Goals

  • ◆ Stable recurring income from crops + artisan goods
  • ◆ All tools upgraded to at least steel/gold
  • ◆ Coop & Barn built with animals producing daily
  • ◆ Mines cleared to floor 80+ (gold ore access)
  • ◆ First kegs & preserves jars operational
  • ◆ 5+ hearts with key villagers; house upgraded once

💰 Artisan Goods = Profit Multiplier

Turning raw crops into artisan goods dramatically increases their value.

🍷 Keg: Fruit → Wine (3× value) 🥒 Jar: Vegetable → Pickles (2×+ value) 🧀 Press: Milk → Cheese 🧈 Churn: Milk → Butter 🐑 Loom: Wool → Cloth
Build a Silo BEFORE you build a Coop or Barn! The silo automatically collects hay when you cut grass. Without it, grass is wasted.
The Secret Woods (requires steel axe) has 6 hardwood stumps that respawn daily — ~12 hardwood/day. Essential for house upgrades!

⛏️ Deeper Mines & New Areas

❄️

🏝️ Ginger Island Overview

After completing the Community Center, Willy's boat takes you to Ginger Island — a tropical paradise with year-round farming and new challenges.

  • 🌴 Island Farm: Any crop, any season — no crows!
  • 🔮 Volcano Dungeon: 10 floors, forge weapons with gems
  • 🌰 Golden Walnuts: Hidden collectibles unlocking new areas
  • 📜 Qi's Walnut Room: Challenges for experienced players
  • 🐟 Island West: Stingray, blue discus, and new forage
🏝️

A whole second farm awaits — with no winter!

Ancient Fruit in the Greenhouse is one of the most profitable setups. Plant once, harvest forever. Turn into wine with kegs for massive passive income all year round.
Obelisks (warp towers) let you teleport to the beach, mountains, desert, and Ginger Island instantly. Expensive but save enormous travel time.

💒 Marriage & Family Life

Reach 10 hearts and give a Bouquet (from Pierre's) to start dating. At 10 hearts, give a Mermaid's Pendant (from the Old Mariner on the beach during rain) to propose. Your spouse helps with chores and gives gifts!

TUTORIALS

Step-by-step guides for every aspect of the game

💧 Planning Your First Sprinkler Layout

  1. 1. Craft Quality Sprinklers (waters 8 tiles, 3×3 area).
  2. 2. Arrange crops in 3×3 blocks with sprinkler in center.
  3. 3. Place blocks side by side — no overlap needed.
  4. 4. Add a Scarecrow (covers large radius) to protect crops.
  5. 5. Upgrade to Iridium Sprinklers (5×5) for huge fields.

3×3 with Quality Sprinkler (blue)

⛏️ How to Prepare for a Mine Run

  1. 1. Check the TV: Good luck days = faster ladder finds!
  2. 2. Pack food: 10–20 healing items (salmonberries, cheese).
  3. 3. Clear inventory: Leave tools you won't need.
  4. 4. Bring bombs for dense rock clusters.
  5. 5. Goal: Reach next elevator checkpoint (5 floors).
  6. 6. Leave by 11 PM to avoid collapsing!
Don't break every rock! Look for ladders near rock clusters. Breaking every rock wastes energy — focus on finding the way down.

🏛️ Community Center Bundle Strategy

  1. 1. Start early! Check bundles as soon as you unlock the CC.
  2. 2. Prioritize the Pantry — unlocks the Greenhouse!
  3. 3. Save one of everything: Keep 1–2 of each crop/forage/fish.
  4. 4. Traveling Cart: Fridays & Sundays for rare items.
  5. 5. Fish Bundle: Save seasonal fish for the Fish Tank bundles.

🐄 Animal Care Basics

  1. 1. Build a Silo first — stores hay from cut grass.
  2. 2. Pet animals daily for higher product quality.
  3. 3. Open doors on sunny days so they eat fresh grass.
  4. 4. Heaters in winter keep animals happy.
  5. 5. Auto-grabbers save time collecting products.

💰 Crop Profit Formula

Profit = (Sell Price − Seed Cost) × Harvests per Season
Regrow crops = (Season Days ÷ Regrow Days) × Sell Price

Wine multiplier: Wine ≈ 3× base crop price. With Artisan: ×4.2 effective multiplier vs raw crop.
Preserves Jar: ≈ 2× base crop price + 50g. Better for low-value / fast-growing crops.
